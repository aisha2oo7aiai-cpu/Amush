Terms and Conditions

Please read these before using Amush.

Version 1.0 · Draft: have this reviewed by a qualified lawyer before launch.

1. What Amush is

Amush is an AI-based support tool for students. It offers non-pharmacological, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) informed support: coping strategies, guided reflection and emotional support delivered through psychological means only.

2. What Amush is not

Amush does not diagnose any condition. It is not a substitute for a licensed psychologist, counselor or psychiatrist. It is not a medical service and does not provide emergency care.

3. No medication or prescriptions

Amush does not prescribe, recommend, adjust or comment on pharmaceutical drugs or dosages. Only a licensed psychiatrist or medical doctor can prescribe medication. Please do not start, stop or change any medication because of anything on this platform.

4. Escalation and referral to a licensed psychiatrist

If our systems or moderators detect signs of high risk or severe distress (for example self-harm, suicidal thoughts, abuse, psychosis-like symptoms, or distress that is not improving), Amush will stop offering ordinary coping support and will:

  • show you emergency and helpline contacts, including Tele-MANAS (14416);
  • strongly advise you to consult a licensed psychiatrist or mental health professional in person; and
  • where our process allows, notify a human responder or helpline contact.

Referral to a licensed psychiatrist is mandatory for sensitive or high-risk cases. Amush will not continue to act as your only source of support in such cases.

5. Eligibility

Only students who meet our eligibility criteria may use Amush. Amush is not suitable for people who are in crisis, are extremely emotionally vulnerable, or need clinical treatment. If that is you, please see a professional in person. If you are under 18, you must have the consent of a parent or guardian.

6. Limits of AI

Amush uses AI that can make mistakes, misunderstand you or give incomplete advice. Treat responses as suggestions, not professional advice. Results vary from person to person and no outcome is guaranteed.

7. Peer community

Peer circles are for mutual support, not treatment. Keep your identity private, be respectful, and do not give medical advice or share another person's information. Moderators may remove content or accounts that break these rules. Peer circles are not available for people experiencing suicidal thoughts; please contact a helpline instead.

8. Your privacy and data

Your conversations may be stored and used to operate, secure and improve the service, including training our AI. We will handle your personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We will not sell your personal data. You may ask us to access or delete your data by contacting us.

9. Free access period

The first 2-3 months are free while we train and evaluate our AI. Terms for later use will be shared with you in advance.

10. Your responsibilities

You agree to give honest information, use Amush only for yourself, not misuse it or other users, and seek professional or emergency help when you need it.

11. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, Amush and its team are not liable for any loss or harm arising from your use of the service or from reliance on its responses. Nothing here limits any right you have that cannot be limited by law.

12. Changes and contact

We may update these Terms. If we make important changes, we will ask for your agreement again. Questions: contact us through amush.com.

In an emergency

If you or someone near you is in danger, call your local emergency number or Tele-MANAS at 14416 (free, 24/7) right now.
